# 07 — Phase 7: Ranked Research Backlog and Index

**Prepared:** 2026-11-04. Per the founder's revised instruction, Phase 7 addresses the highest-value open topics **once** and then stops. It is not an open-ended research loop.

## Ranking method
Each candidate topic was scored on (a) how likely the answer is to change the go/no-go decision and (b) whether desk research can answer it, as opposed to needing a pilot. Topics that only a pilot can answer are listed as validation experiments in the founder brief, not researched further.

| Rank | Topic | Could change go/no-go? | Desk-answerable? | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Demand ceiling:** how many people could ever use a Chicago pouch price site? | **Yes, strongly** | Partly (top-down from PMI volumes) | ✅ Memo 08 |
| 2 | **Competitors and distribution channels** (Nicotinly, PouchBuddy, app stores, SEO) | Yes | Yes | ✅ Memo 08 |
| 3 | **Price dispersion and freshness:** is local dispersion large and persistent; how often do prices change; the 15→20 can transition | **Yes** (gate G1) | Partly; the decisive number needs the manual pilot | ✅ Memo 09 |
| 4 | **Alternative data sources** cheaper than calling | Yes (removes the legal risk) | Yes | ✅ Memo 09 §4 |
| 5 | **12/24/36-month financials, runway, founder time, kill criteria** | Yes | Yes (with assumptions) | ✅ Memo 10 |
| 6 | Consumer price site vs. B2B dataset strategy | Yes | Mostly covered in Phase 3 | Folded into Memos 08 and 10 |
| 7 | Retailer incentives and self-reporting | Moderate | Covered in Phase 5 §6.3 | No new memo |
| 8 | Brand/trademark/domain due diligence; adjacent age-restricted products | Low | Partly (USPTO search needs interactive access) | Noted in brief; not researched further |
| 9 | Customer willingness to revisit; alerts and retention economics | Moderate | Weak without data | Pilot metric (G3) |
| 10 | Chicago flavor-ordinance status for pouches | Moderate (affects listings) | Partly | Recorded as OPEN in register; a 2020 vape-only flavor ban is verified; no 2025–26 pouch ban found |
